# Code of conduct

UniverseView is a small hobby project made for people who build worlds and stories. Everyone who opens an issue, sends a pull request or helps translate is welcome here, whatever their experience, language, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not programmers, and English is not everyone's first language.
- Say what you need, and listen to what others need. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding something was meant badly.
- Give credit, and take feedback on your own work gracefully.
- Keep issues about the project. Do not post other people's private information.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Sexualised language or attention, or any unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or anything else you would not say in front of the whole community.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@StoutCrow](https://github.com/StoutCrow)) may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules. To report something, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or open a private security advisory on this repository if it must stay private (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled fairly and kept private.

## Scope

This applies in all of this repository's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
